01

주어진 글 다음에 이어질 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?

118 words

Sea otters spend most of their lives in cold water, yet they have no thick layer of fat to keep them warm. Instead, they rely on fur — the densest coat of any animal on Earth.

(A)

That trapped air, not the fur itself, is what holds the heat in. The system works only while the coat stays clean.

(B)

A single square inch can hold more than a million hairs, which trap a thin layer of air against the skin.

(C)

So an otter grooms almost constantly, rolling and blowing air back into its fur between meals. When oil coats the hairs, they clump together and the air escapes.

02

글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳은?

But the protection disappears the moment the coat is fouled.

Fur alone does not keep a sea otter warm. ① The hairs trap a layer of air, and it is that air which holds the heat. ② Grooming keeps the layer in place, so the animal spends hours a day at it. ③ Oil makes the hairs stick together and the air escapes. ④ Rescuers therefore wash oiled otters before anything else. ⑤
