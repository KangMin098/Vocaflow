## Relations, contradiction, and narrow interpretation

All77 tests were read against their anchors and the fixed item/path. Relation existence and necessity remain separate. Generic “cannot complete this explanation without this link” assertions do not prove that the same supplied information cannot identify the same candidate through an alternative operation.

* A insertion declares causal required but marks its causal test necessary=false, with two-reference alternative. A irrelevant-sentence path likewise declares causal required but marks its test necessary=false. These are internal set/test defects, independent of A/B agreement.
* B irrelevant-sentence necessity overstates AI: s5 and s7 still oppose realistic-representation strengthening when the causal explanation of photography's effect is not used. Necessity to reproduce the full causal explanation is a different target from identifying4.
* A title's example necessity is unresolved: current440 plus alternative frequencies/historical standards already distinguish title1 without using later units as examples. A harder comparison is not impossibility without the link.
* Summary's elaboration/contrast need is unresolved: persistence can be independently recognized from s2, limiting conditions from s5/s7, then mapped to two slots. Reading multiple units or joining slots does not alone prove using the asserted contrast relation is necessary.
* Topic raters test different causal anchors, A s4/s5→s6 versus B s3→s6. Lack of private financial benefit alone does not establish overall economic inefficiency; higher nonmarket value does. Matching causal labels can conceal different propositions.
* Ordering question→“this question” and answer→ending examples are persuasive coherence constraints, especially for excluding B-first options. C-A-B is weaker than C-B-A but can place an illustrative answer before a general restatement; “less apt” needs no logical-impossibility label. In insertion, later reader introduction impairs ordinary forward reference, but cataphora/accommodated addressees complicate an absolute contradiction label.
* Historical alternative tuning does not contradict a newly popular trend. Persistent craftsmanship need not logically exclude declining prevalence. Unexpected accurate reporting need not universally require courage. The lexical task's intended opposition is a pragmatic best-fit judgment, and its bridge should be disclosed rather than encoded as external_assumption=null plus universal entailment.

These are claim-level audits. The structured record contains **9 defect findings and10 unresolved findings** across selected claims and records. The19 findings are not19 wrong items; multiple findings can concern one item/path, and broader record defects can affect multiple paths. No official answer was established wrong. All eleven full-source best answers have narrow support.

## Allowable claims and R0/R1 boundary

Allowed: descriptive sealed AI agreement on this purposive sample; the listed narrow original-source best-answer accounts; derived-chart numeric verification; explicitly conditional unique-error/unique-mismatch detection; and R0 candidate teaching contracts or future falsification tasks.

Blocked: complete support-target equivalence; universally valid restricted reading or minimum information; general required-relation inventories; expansion based on numeric agreement alone; population frequency; causal improvement from v1.2; actual learner selective reading, mastery, score prediction, total required practice, automatic prescription, or instructional effects. Reading a whole source and then choosing a window is a logical hypothesis, not evidence of students using that window.

R0 is a candidate claim or educational contract. R1 is an AI account bound to the supplied original, with claim-specific limitations. Neither is human content approval/publication (R2), direct learner task implementation (R3), observed student performance (R4), or teaching effect (R5). This review reaches no R2–R5 evidence. PRODUCT_HANDOFF_READY is false for claims requiring those levels. The independent pilot logic audit is completed; the strong validity claims remain blocked while other separately scoped TASK/principle/education work may continue.
