# Atomic production snapshot review brief — 2026-10-08

Goal: Close the caller-evidence boundary for single/multi-grade synthetic production and prepare a transactionally captured DB evidence set. Do not claim operational production verification without a real admitted child.

Acceptance review:
- `reading_production_group` is the administrator-registered grade/order/article/audit/item selection; revisions are monotonic.
- `_reading_production_evidence` locks group, authority, order, audit, child, source, item and answer rows in one RPC transaction and fails on rights, readiness, Gold-S/seed, revocation, expiry, hash, and lineage mismatch.
- Capture writes the exact JSONB evidence and SHA-256 with a short expiry; finalization re-collects under locks and records one exact HTML digest. A replay or changed dependency fails.
- Publication stores the exact HTML under the same transaction as a new evidence check; serving recomputes current evidence and blocks an artifact after later revocation or mutation. No external file is an authorized publication.
- A second active administrator, distinct from the group registrant, approves the exact finalized snapshot ID/hash and output SHA-256. The approval records a hash of the reviewed evidence excluding the approval fields. The publication RPC rejects changed evidence or other HTML, even when called directly with `service_role`. A group revision clears the approval and invalidates old snapshots.
- Unit HTML is an editorial output, not a DB source of truth. The system binds its exact bytes to a finalized DB snapshot and requires a different active administrator to inspect and approve them before publication. It does not automatically prove that every question is visibly present in the layout; that remains an explicit editorial review obligation and prevents a claim of `production_verified` until real operational E2E.
- The DB collector verifies the stored Gold-S/seed Ed25519 signatures against the current policy, recomputes current item/review and sealed item-set digests, and locks item state/review tables so missing rows cannot appear mid-capture. The JavaScript runner consumes only DB-returned group/evidence, independently checks the same order/grade and item/explanation bytes, then returns an unpublished manifest with snapshot/output hashes.
- The importer stores the signed Gold-S/seed bundle, public-key policy and calculated earliest expiry in the child provenance. Capture re-verifies both signatures and expiry using DB-returned rows; rendered explanations must equal DB `answer_key.explanation_ko` or `rationale_ko`.
- The existing non-atomic `render-volume.mjs --product-order` adds an unpublished marker; the CLI rejects `--production`/`--publish` for every render.
