What a Hat Can Tell

이 단위의 도움

I took the tattered thing in my hands and turned it over rather unhappily. It was a very ordinary black hat of the usual round shape, hard and very worn. The lining had once been red silk, but it was badly discoloured.

“I can’t see anything,” I said, handing it back to my friend.

“On the contrary, Watson, you can see everything. But you fail to reason from what you see. You are too timid in drawing your conclusions.”
