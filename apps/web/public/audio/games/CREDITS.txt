v07.5 — CHIPTUNE REPLACED
-------------------------
The previous set leaned on 8-bit square-wave tracks (8bit Dungeon Level,
Bit Quest, Bit Shift, Awesome Call ...). They read as thin "PC beep" music and
clashed with the weight of a study space. Every game now has its own
orchestral / cinematic / ambient track — no track is reused.
