Attribution is also surfaced in-app on the /arcade page footer.

Processed for Vocaflow (no melodic content altered):
  · A 116 s window is chosen automatically from each original — intro (first
    15%) and outro (last 8%) excluded, then the window scoring highest on
    "loud but steady" (mean RMS − 0.6 × SD of the per-second envelope).
  · That window is rebuilt as a SEAMLESS 110 s loop:
        out = concat( acrossfade(tail 6 s, head 6 s), body 98 s )
    Both join points (body→tail, head→body) are original, uncut continuity,
    so the loop has no hole and no click. The previous build faded 3 s in /
    5 s out, which punched an 8-second gap into the music every 110 seconds.
  · EBU R128 loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS (TP -1.5 dBTP) so no game is
    louder than another, then encoded to VBR MP3 (-q:a 5), 44.1 kHz stereo.

v07.6 — KENNEY SYNTH BLIPS REPLACED BY RECORDED SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------------
Measured (FFT, see the analysis notes below) on the outgoing Kenney set:
all six files were MONO, carried 0–0.6% of their energy above 8 kHz, and two
of them (correct, complete) had a spectral flatness of 0.0000. Those are
band-limited synthesised tones — the "just computer sounds" complaint was
literally correct. Every replacement is a stereo recording with inharmonic
partials, a natural decay and real high-frequency air.

  correct   ← "Achievement bell"              0.65 s  peak -3 dBFS
  wrong     ← "Wood hard hit"                 0.30 s  peak -8 dBFS
  combo     ← "Fairy arcade sparkle"          0.95 s  peak -6 dBFS
  click     ← "Typewriter soft click"         0.15 s  peak -9 dBFS
  coin      ← "Clinking coins"                0.45 s  peak -6 dBFS
  complete  ← "Orchestra triumphant trumpets" 2.80 s  peak -3 dBFS

Processing: trimmed to the window shown (chosen off a 0.1 s-resolution RMS
envelope so no attack is clipped and no tail is cut mid-decay), 8 ms fade in
against click noise, fade out over the last 0.1–0.9 s, then two-pass peak
normalisation to the target above. Peak targets encode the intended hierarchy
(완주 > 정답 > 코인·콤보 > 오답 > 클릭); the per-call gains in gamekit's
useSfx sit on top of them.

wrong is a wooden knock rather than a buzzer on purpose — Empathetic
Feedback (CLAUDE.md 디자인 철학 3): 오답에 비난조를 싣지 않는다.
