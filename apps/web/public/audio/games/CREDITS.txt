v07.7 — SELECTED BY MEASUREMENT, NOT BY TITLE
----------------------------------------------
Brief: 웅장하면서 긴장감과 긴박감이 있어야 하고, 빠른 템포가 필요하다.

The v07.6 set failed that brief, and the reason was measurable. 118 candidate
tracks (Scott Buckley 72 + Alexander Nakarada 46) were analysed for:

  bpm       tempo, from autocorrelation of the spectral-flux onset envelope
  onset/s   attacks per second — the direct proxy for 긴박감
  pulse     sharpness of the autocorrelation peak. ~1.0 means the beat is
            indistinguishable from noise: the track is an ambient pad and has
            no drive at all, whatever its title suggests
  low%      share of onset energy below 150 Hz — kick / low strings, the punch
  full%     share of time RMS stays above 60% of peak — 웅장 (a full tutti)
  tension   time-variance of the 2–6 kHz band — tremolo, staccato, dissonance

Most of the Scott Buckley library sits at pulse ≈ 1.0–1.3: beautiful, but
ambient. That is why the previous set read as calm rather than urgent.
Alexander Nakarada's epic/cinematic catalogue measured far higher on every
drive axis, so 16 of 19 slots moved there. Every selected track is
129–161 BPM with a clearly detectable pulse.


BEAT-ALIGNED SEAMLESS LOOPS
---------------------------
Each track is cut to a whole number of BARS at its measured tempo, so the
loop point is phase-coherent:

    out = concat( acrossfade(tail 1 bar, head 1 bar), body )

Because loopLen is an exact multiple of the bar, the tail (at start+loopLen)
carries the same beat phase as the head (at start) — the crossfade lands beat
on beat instead of smearing two offset grooves into a flam. Both join points
(body→tail, head→body) are original, uncut continuity. Loops run 109.5–110.6 s
depending on tempo (59–74 bars).

⚠️ Two build traps, both silent, both now guarded by an assertion that the
   output length equals loopLen:
   1. Splitting one input with `asplit=3` and feeding three `atrim`s into
      `acrossfade` makes the crossfade vanish entirely — the trims deadlock in
      lockstep and acrossfade receives an empty stream. Open head/tail/body as
      three separate `-i` inputs instead.
   2. A segment extracted with `-t X` can come back a hair shorter than X
      because of MP3 frame granularity, and then `acrossfade=d=X` silently
      drops the crossfade as well. Extract X+0.4 s and `atrim` to exactly X.
   In both cases the file still serves HTTP 200 and still plays; it is just
   1 bar short and clicks on every loop. Nothing catches it but the length
   assertion and tests/e2e/12-arcade-audio.spec.ts.

Loudness: EBU R128 to -16 LUFS (TP -1.5 dBTP) across all tracks so no game is
louder than another. Encoding: VBR MP3 (-q:a 5), 44.1 kHz stereo. 33.3 MB total.
No melodic content altered.

v07.6 — KENNEY SYNTH BLIPS REPLACED BY RECORDED SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------------
Measured (FFT) on the outgoing Kenney set: all six files were MONO, carried
0–0.6% of their energy above 8 kHz, and two of them (correct, complete) had a
spectral flatness of 0.0000. Those are band-limited synthesised tones — the
"just computer sounds" complaint was literally correct. Every replacement is a
stereo recording with inharmonic partials, a natural decay and real
high-frequency air.

  correct   ← "Achievement bell"              0.65 s  peak -3 dBFS
  wrong     ← "Wood hard hit"                 0.30 s  peak -8 dBFS
  combo     ← "Fairy arcade sparkle"          0.95 s  peak -6 dBFS
  click     ← "Typewriter soft click"         0.15 s  peak -9 dBFS
  coin      ← "Clinking coins"                0.45 s  peak -6 dBFS
  complete  ← "Orchestra triumphant trumpets" 2.80 s  peak -3 dBFS

Processing: trimmed to the window shown (chosen off a 0.1 s-resolution RMS
envelope so no attack is clipped and no tail is cut mid-decay), 8 ms fade in
against click noise, fade out over the last 0.1–0.9 s, then two-pass peak
normalisation to the target above. Peak targets encode the intended hierarchy
(완주 > 정답 > 코인·콤보 > 오답 > 클릭); the per-call gains in gamekit's
useSfx sit on top of them.

wrong is a wooden knock rather than a buzzer on purpose — Empathetic
Feedback (CLAUDE.md 디자인 철학 3): 오답에 비난조를 싣지 않는다.
